Admin

New rule

Only you can make someone a moderator. You send the invite, they accept. Nobody else can add or remove moderators.

  • Invite from the members list, choose what they can do
  • The left phone is you. The right phone is the person you invite.
  • Approve staff roles, set underage visibility, change the club code

12Section

Weekly content pack

Two separate sites

The Éanna Hub is public, for the whole club. The Éanna Media Calendar stays private, for the media team only. They have different addresses and logins. The only connection is one way: the hub drops the weekly pack into the calendar's For approval tab.

  • Every Monday the hub builds the pack: carousel, results graphics, best answer, top photos, milestones
  • You choose what goes, then send
  • Nothing in the Media Calendar ever appears on the hub
  • Underage photos only go if consent was ticked

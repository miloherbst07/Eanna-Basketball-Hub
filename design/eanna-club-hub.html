1What it does

Eight parts. They can all be built at once, but it's better to launch them in stages (see section 9).

Score-sheets and stats

  • Coaches and managers upload a photo of the score-sheet from their phone.
  • The site reads the running-score column, works out points per shirt number and matches numbers to the team roster.
  • A backup box asks for the final score and top 3 scorers, in case the photo can't be read.
  • Every sheet waits for a quick check before it counts. Admins can edit or delete afterwards.
  • National League games can use official Basketball Ireland stats where they exist.

Public stats website

  • This week: every result, top scorers, top performers split male and female.
  • Any past week of the season.
  • Team pages: results, win–loss, points for and against, top scorers, team photo.
  • Player pages: games, total points, average, season high.
  • Leaderboards: season top scorers and best single games.
  • Each season is archived and stays viewable.

Underage visibility controls

  • At underage level, players with zero points never appear in public game stats.
  • Player toggle: a parent (or an 18+ player) can hide a player's stats everywhere.
  • Team toggle: show full stats, results only, or hide the team completely.
  • Admins always see the full data. Hidden is never deleted.

Photos

  • Upload team photos, match photos and event photos alongside score-sheets.
  • Galleries for each team plus a club gallery, sorted by week.
  • Every photo is approved before it goes public, so consent can be checked.
  • No name tags on underage photos.

Team news

  • A news feed for each team and one for the whole club: birthdays, exams, milestones, new coaches, wins.
  • Approved before posting. Reactions only (👏 🎉 🏀), with no comments.
  • A report button on every post.

Community board

  • The moderator posts a question each week, e.g. "What's the hardest part about underage basketball?"
  • Members answer and react. Adults can reply to each other.
  • There's a separate under-18 board where they answer but can't reply to each other.
  • Questions can be scheduled weeks ahead, and the best answers pinned.

Staff rooms

  • Private chat rooms for Coaches, Team managers, Committee and Moderators, plus an optional All staff room.
  • Only verified adults you approve get in.
  • Threads, pinned messages, file sharing, announcements, and read-only rooms if needed.
  • Rooms can be archived at the end of a season.

Social tools (for the media team)

  • Weekly carousel: one button makes "Top Performers This Week" (male and female) in Éanna branding.
  • Templates for final scores, player of the week, milestones and shout-outs.
  • Any result, photo, news post or answer can be sent to the Media Calendar in one tap.

Moderators: moderators can edit everything on the hub, and every edit is logged with who made it and when. Only the admin (Milo) can make someone a moderator. The admin sends an invite, the person accepts, and nobody else can add or remove moderators.

3Signing up

People sign up once and stay signed in on that phone. There are no passwords to remember.

How it works

  • Viewing the site needs no sign-up at all.
  • To join, people enter the club code (shared in team WhatsApp groups), then sign in with Google, Apple or an email link.
  • If the code leaks, an admin changes it in one place.

Verified roles

  • Coach, Team manager, Committee and Moderator show as "pending" until an admin approves them. They then get a ✓ badge.
  • Team managers can approve their own team's player sign-ups, so admins aren't checking every one.
  • Under-18 accounts can never be given a staff role.
  • At the end of the season, everyone can be moved up an age group in one go.

4Moderation

The aim is to stop most problems before anything is posted, then make dealing with the rest a one-tap job.

Before anything is posted

  • Approval modes for each board:
    • All posts held: nothing is posted until approved. This is the default for under-18 boards.
    • New users held: a username's first 3–5 posts need approval.
    • Open: posts go live straight away and get cleaned up afterwards.
  • Word filter: swearing, slurs and insults are blocked or held. Admins can add their own words.
  • Personal details blocked automatically: phone numbers, emails, @handles, addresses and links. This stops people moving conversations off the site.
  • Username check: rude or fake names such as "Coach_Official" are rejected.
  • Slow mode (e.g. one post every 2 minutes) and a post length limit.
  • Opening hours: boards lock automatically at night, e.g. 10pm–8am.
  • On/off switch for any board, or for replies on any question.

When something goes wrong

  • Report button on every post and photo. Two reports hide it until a moderator checks it.
  • Actions: hide, edit, warn, mute (1 day, 7 days or indefinitely), or ban. A ban removes the username and blocks that phone from making a new one.
  • Moderators get a notification on their phone when something is reported or waiting.

Steps for rule-breaking

1 · HidePost removed from view
2 · WarnModerator message shown to the user
3 · MuteRead only for 1 or 7 days
4 · BanAccount and device removed

Anything involving a child's safety skips straight to the club's children's officer, not the steps above (see section 5).

Moderator screen

  • One queue for posts, photos, score-sheets, reports, new usernames and role requests.
  • Full history for each user, including hidden posts.
  • Action log: every action by every moderator, with time and reason. This is the record if a parent or the club ever asks.
  • Moderator permissions are set individually (e.g. can hide but not ban).

Staff rooms

Staff rooms are for trusted, verified adults, so messages post instantly and personal details are allowed (for match-day phone numbers). Admins still control who's in each room, and removals are logged.

5Underage and safeguarding

How the hub fits Irish safeguarding rules and Basketball Ireland's guidance. The club's children's officer should review and sign off on this before launch.

What's required or expectedHow the hub handles it
No private contact between adults and children (Sport Ireland / Basketball Ireland safeguarding guidance)No private messages anywhere. Everything is public on the board and logged. Under-18s can't reply to anyone, and staff rooms are adults only.
Parental consent for under-16s online (GDPR digital age of consent in Ireland is 16)Under-16 accounts only activate after a linked parent approves them. Parents control their child's stats and can delete the account.
Consent for photos of children (club photo policy)All photos are approved before going public. Moderators check consent, and underage photos are never name-tagged.
Collect as little as possible about children (GDPR data minimisation)No date of birth, school, address, phone or photo on profiles. Under-18 profiles show username, team and role only.
Don't embarrass or single out childrenZero-point players are hidden at underage level, and there are player and team toggles. Leaderboards can be switched off for younger age groups.
Child Safeguarding Statement (Children First Act 2015)The club's statement should be updated to mention the hub. The rules page links to it and names the children's officer.
Vetted adults in roles with access to children (Garda vetting)Recommendation: only Garda-vetted adults become moderators of under-18 boards. Admins approve every staff role.
Clear way to raise a concernEvery page has a "Raise a concern" link going to the children's officer (not just to moderators). Report button on every post.
Keep recordsHidden posts and the action log are kept, so evidence isn't lost if something needs to be escalated.

If a moderator sees something that worries them about a child's safety, they hide it, don't delete it, and pass it straight to the children's officer, who follows the club's reporting procedure (including Tusla or Gardaí where required). The hub's job is to record and escalate, not to investigate.

6Data and privacy

  • Where data is kept: Google Firebase, set to an EU data centre.
  • Privacy notice: a short, plain-English page shown at sign-up, covering what's stored, why, who sees it and how to delete it. The club is the data controller.
  • Deleting an account: any member (or the parent of an under-16) can delete their account from settings. Their profile and posts are removed. Game stats stay as part of match results but are hidden from the public.
  • How long things are kept: inactive accounts are deleted after 2 seasons. Score-sheet photos are kept for the season. Moderation records are kept as long as the club's safeguarding policy says.
  • Who can see hidden data: admins only. Every admin view of hidden data is logged.
  • Security: sign-in by Google, Apple or email link, so the site never stores passwords. Database rules stop anyone reading or editing what their role doesn't allow.
  • Data protection impact assessment: a short one is recommended, because the hub handles children's data. I can draft it.

8Cost, app and notifications

App on iPhone and Android

  • It installs from the browser with the Éanna crest as the icon. On iPhone: Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen. On Android, Chrome offers "Install app".
  • Push notifications work on Android straight away. On iPhone they work once the hub is added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer), and the hub shows a short guide.
  • App Store and Google Play versions can come later. That costs about €99 a year for Apple and a one-off $25 for Google, and needs app review.

Notifications

  • Notification types: results, weekly top performers, team news and photos, weekly question, reactions, staff room messages, score-sheet reminders for coaches, moderation alerts, club announcements.
  • Each person chooses by type and by team.
  • Quiet hours are 10pm–8am by default, and always on for under-18s. Under-18s only get team and club notifications.
  • A weekly email roundup is available for people who don't turn on push.

Cost and tech

  • Running cost: €0 at club size on Firebase's free tier and GitHub Pages. Reading score-sheets with AI may cost a few cent per sheet, or nothing if done by hand.
  • Works on any phone with no app store download, and can be added to the home screen like an app.
  • Same setup as the Éanna Media Calendar, so the two can link: results and photos go straight into the content planner.
  • It can live at its own address or be linked from eannabasketball.ie.

9Build stages

Stage 1

Score-sheets, stats website, underage visibility controls, admin approvals, weekly carousel builder. This gives you the weekly top performers post straight away.

Stage 2

Sign-up and profiles (with badges and interests), team news, photo galleries, notifications.

Stage 3

Community board and weekly question, staff rooms, full moderation tools and action log.

Each stage can be tested with a few teams before it's opened to the whole club.
